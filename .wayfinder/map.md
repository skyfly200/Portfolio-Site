## Decisions

### D1 — Primary income anchor ✅ DECIDED
**Decision:** Web3 freelance is the anchor. $150–175/hr, 10 hrs/week.
**Evidence:** Already have one client at $150/hr. Prior Lead Web3/Solidity offers at $250k. ArtBlocks + TinyBoxes credibility. 10 hrs/week hits $80k floor.

### D2 — SparkFun application ✅ IN PROGRESS
**Decision:** Apply. Embedded Hardware Engineer ($75–90k) is strong alignment with SparkFun ArtNet LED controller + LED Rose projects, Nathan Siedel PR acceptance. Use as stable bridge while freelance scales. Not a ceiling.

### D3 — Product sales channel ✅ DECIDED
**Decision:** Local-only. Root Kava Bar (First Friday), art gallery, festivals, friends' events. No Etsy/shipping until demand proves it.

### D4 — Web3 client strategy ✅ DECIDED
**Decision:** Current client = hourly only, no retainer pitch (difficult, doesn't recognize value). New clients only for retainer/larger engagements. Warm outreach to new contacts, not past clients with bad endings.

### D5 — Agentic coding positioning ✅ DECIDED
**Decision:** Market as "AI Agent Developer" at $155–235/hr. Custom harnesses + workflows as differentiator. Use Claude Pro + Gemini + Copilot + Qwen Coder strategically.
